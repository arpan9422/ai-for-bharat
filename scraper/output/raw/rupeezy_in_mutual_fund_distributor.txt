Q1. What is a mutual fund distributor?

Ans. A mutual fund distributor or mutual fund agent is an individual or a financial intermediary who helps investors buy/sell and manage their mutual fund investments.

Q2. What is the eligibility to become a mutual fund distributor?

Ans. A mutual fund distributor operates under the purview of SEBI and AMFI. The following eligibility criteria must be met to become a mutual fund distributor: Qualification: Age 18+; There is no specific degree required to become an MFD Certification: Must pass NISM-Series-V-A certification test, as mandated by SEBI. ARN (AMFI Registration Number) is issued upon completion of this certification. Empanelment: After receiving the ARN, candidates can empanel themselves as mutual fund distributor with Rupeezy. Code of Conduct: Mutual fund distributors must adhere to stipulated rules and code of conduct, any violation can lead to de-registration and penal action.

Qualification: Age 18+; There is no specific degree required to become an MFD

Certification: Must pass NISM-Series-V-A certification test, as mandated by SEBI. ARN (AMFI Registration Number) is issued upon completion of this certification.

Empanelment: After receiving the ARN, candidates can empanel themselves as mutual fund distributor with Rupeezy.

Code of Conduct: Mutual fund distributors must adhere to stipulated rules and code of conduct, any violation can lead to de-registration and penal action.

Q3. What are the roles and responsibilities of an MF distributor?

Ans. MF distributors are expected to fulfil following roles and responsibilities: Investor Education: The main responsibility of an MFD is to educate investors about mutual funds, types of MF investments, their workings, past performance, risks, features and overall benefits. Execution Assistance: MFD assists in executing the purchase of mutual funds in a timely and efficient manner. MFD also assists the investor in understanding the Rupeezy product such that they can manage their mutual funds.
