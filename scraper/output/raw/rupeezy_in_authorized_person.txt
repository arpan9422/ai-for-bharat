Q1. Who can become a Rupeezy Authorized Person?

Ans. Anyone with a passion for finance and a drive to grow, be it a mutual fund distributor, financial advisor, financial influencer, part-time trader or full-time professional. No prior experience required, the person should be 12th passed.

Q2. Is there any joining fee?

Ans. No. Rupeezy's AP program starts with zero joining fee. There are the only registration charges by the NSE. Our subscription fee starting at Rs 2.5K can be deducted from the commissions earned.

Q3. What is the difference between an Authorized Person and a Sub-Broker?

Ans. They are functionally similar. Authorized Person (AP) is the SEBI-approved model that replaced the sub-broker system after 2009. Rupeezy follows this updated, compliant model.

Q4. How do I earn as an AP?

Ans. You earn 100% of the brokerage generated by your referred clients. The more your clients trade or invest, the more you earn.

Q5. When and how do I receive payouts?

Ans. Payouts are processed daily and can be instantly withdrawn via the RISE Portal.
